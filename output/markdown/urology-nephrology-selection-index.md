## Scope decisions

The additional set retains pelvic-floor/perineal anatomy, vulval lymphatics, a flank-pain pelvic differential and a broader EPO question for optional use. General gynaecology, obstetrics, menstrual physiology, contraception, general STI/HIV teaching without a specific urinary or genital-tract complication, and incidental mentions of renal drugs in unrelated distractors were excluded. Isolated adrenal pubertal physiology was also excluded because the question does not test renal function or urological management.

Mixed questions are retained in full, including their non-urological choices. Main Q94 is retained for haematuria despite its haematology focus; Main Q24 is retained for its renal-transplant pharmacology relevance.

No independent answer validation was performed. Selection is based on the supplied question content.
