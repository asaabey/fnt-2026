OPTIONAL VOICE-OVER OUTLINE

Baseline
Vasopressin regulates collecting-duct water permeability by controlling the
amount of AQP2 in the apical membrane.

Water load
Adding water lowers plasma osmolality and dilutes plasma sodium. The sodium
symbols stay the same while the amount of water increases.

Hormonal response
Lower plasma osmolality suppresses vasopressin release. More AQP2 is retrieved
from the apical membrane into intracellular vesicles, reducing water permeability.

Renal response
Less water leaves the collecting duct. More remains in the tubular fluid,
so urine volume rises and urine osmolality falls.

Negative feedback
Excreting the excess water limits dilution of plasma sodium and helps move
its concentration towards baseline.

Take-home
Water load, lower vasopressin, lower collecting-duct water permeability,
and increased excretion of dilute urine.
