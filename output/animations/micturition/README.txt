COORDINATED MICTURITION

A captioned, silent animation of normal storage and voluntary voiding in an
adult with intact neurological pathways. It accompanies the micturition
question in the renal teaching set and uses the established visual style.

Sequence
Storage: relaxed detrusor, closed outlet and contracted external sphincter.
Filling: sensory information reaches brain circuits; higher centres permit
voiding when appropriate.
Switch: pontine coordination increases sacral pelvic parasympathetic output
and reduces hypogastric sympathetic and pudendal somatic output.
Emptying: outlet relaxation accompanies the onset of detrusor contraction;
pressure in the bladder drives urine through a low-resistance outlet.

Visual design
Dark #10151F background, Avenir Next, white captions, muted anatomical labels,
teal, gold and coral. Each nerve label sits above its own pathway. Muscle
state labels sit beside the relevant structure. There are no week, learning-
objective, course-branding or exam-answer labels.
Gold denotes the sympathetic lane and bladder-outlet smooth muscle; coral
denotes the pudendal lane and external striated sphincter. Teal denotes the
pelvic parasympathetic lane, urine and coordinated voiding.

Physiological interpretation and limits
The three lanes show net efferent activity, not all individual neurons,
synapses, ganglia or receptor mechanisms. The vertical connector is an
organisational schematic, not a spinal tract or a direct connection from
pontine neurons to each peripheral nerve. S2–S4 is shown for the pelvic
parasympathetic and pudendal somatic pathways.
The filling-signal arc summarises an ascending sensory process. Actual
signals pass through spinal and supraspinal relays, including the
periaqueductal grey. Fading this explanatory arc does not imply that
sensory feedback ceases during voiding.
Higher-centre permission and pontine coordination are distinct components
of voluntary voiding; the animation does not imply conscious direct control
of individual autonomic nerves.
Sympathetic activity supports storage by relaxing the detrusor and promoting
outlet smooth-muscle tone. Pelvic parasympathetic activity contracts the
detrusor and contributes to urethral smooth-muscle relaxation. Reduced
sympathetic and pudendal activity also favours outlet relaxation. Inhibiting
pudendal motor activity allows external sphincter relaxation; it is not an
active inhibitory peripheral nerve signal to the muscle.
The bladder neck/outlet is drawn separately from the external sphincter.
It is not intended to depict a discrete anatomical internal sphincter in
all sexes. Pelvic-floor detail, ureters and upper urinary tract are omitted.
Outlet relaxation begins before the prominent contraction/emptying phase;
normal coordination is more complex than a strict step-by-step switch.
Bladder dimensions, nerve pulses, residual urine dots and stream speed are
qualitative. They do not measure activity, pressure, volume or completeness
of emptying. No abdominal straining is required in the illustrated sequence.

Reproduce
With Python 3.13 and the pinned Manim dependency installed:
manim -r 1920,1080 --fps 30 --media_dir /tmp/micturition-render micturition.py Micturition -o micturition.mp4

For fast layout review, set MICTURITION_REVIEW=storage or =voiding and use
Manim's -s option to save the respective final frame.
