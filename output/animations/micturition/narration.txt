OPTIONAL VOICE-OVER OUTLINE

Storage
During filling, the detrusor remains relaxed. Sympathetic activity supports
storage, while pudendal activity keeps the external sphincter contracted.

Permission to void
Bladder filling sends sensory information to the brain. When voiding is
appropriate, higher centres allow pontine circuits to coordinate the switch.

Neural switch
Sacral pelvic parasympathetic activity increases. Sympathetic and pudendal
activity decrease.

Coordinated emptying
The outlet relaxes as detrusor contraction begins. Reduced pudendal drive
allows the external sphincter to relax. Bladder pressure then drives urine
through the open, low-resistance outlet.

Take-home
Effective micturition depends on coordination: the bladder contracts while
the outlet relaxes.
