MECHANISMS AND SOURCES
Evidence checked 10 November 2026.

This is an educational synthesis. The bar states are illustrative choices,
not values taken from the cited studies or numerical treatment predictions.
The preferential vessel effects are qualified by their clinical setting.

physiology — renal vascular regulation, angiotensin II and perfusion:
Renal Autoregulation in Health and Disease.
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4551215/

aki-guidance — NSAIDs, perfusion dependence and interacting medicines:
NICE. Acute kidney injury: prevention, detection and management.
https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng148/chapter/Recommendations
The associated full guideline describes prostaglandin-dependent afferent
vasodilation and angiotensin-dependent efferent constriction.
https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng148/evidence/full-guideline-pdf-7019266862

nkf-raas — ACE inhibitors/ARBs, long-term benefit and combination AKI risk:
National Kidney Foundation. ACE inhibitors and ARBs.
https://www.kidney.org/kidney-topics/ace-inhibitors-and-arbs

kdigo-ckd — initial SGLT2-related eGFR dip and kidney-protective treatment:
KDIGO 2024 CKD guideline, especially practice point 3.7.3.
https://kdigo.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/KDIGO-2024-CKD-Guideline.pdf
Executive summary:
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.kint.2023.10.016

cherney — classic hyperfiltration physiology:
Cherney et al. Renal hemodynamic effect of sodium-glucose cotransporter 2
inhibition in patients with type 1 diabetes mellitus. Circulation (2014).
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24334175/
https://doi.org/10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.113.005081

red-trial — post-glomerular interpretation in a human type 2 diabetes study:
The renal hemodynamic effects of the SGLT2 inhibitor dapagliflozin are
caused by post-glomerular vasodilatation rather than pre-glomerular
vasoconstriction in metformin-treated patients with type 2 diabetes in
the randomized, double-blind RED trial.
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31791665/
Vessel-resistance interpretations in these human studies are inferred
from physiological measurements rather than direct visual measurement
of every afferent/efferent arteriolar diameter.

sglt2-review — context and differences in arteriolar effects:
Renoprotective Mechanism of Sodium-Glucose Cotransporter 2 Inhibitors:
Focusing on Renal Hemodynamics (2022).
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35929172/
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9353563/

Clinical outcome context and expected initial dip:
KDIGO 2022 Diabetes Management in CKD guideline, practice point 1.3.5.
https://kdigo.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/KDIGO-2022-Diabetes-Guideline.pdf

The SGLT2 presentation deliberately distinguishes the model of feedback-
mediated afferent constriction from the post-glomerular dilation supported
in some settings. It does not equate SGLT2 therapy with NSAID toxicity.

The review literature continues to evolve; this scene is not an exhaustive
account of tubular, metabolic or microvascular kidney-protective mechanisms.

cni-review — afferent-predominant acute toxicity, with mixed vascular effects:
Calcineurin Inhibitor Associated Nephrotoxicity in Kidney Transplantation—
A Transplant Nephrologist's Perspective (2025).
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12005075/
The Many Faces of Calcineurin Inhibitor Toxicity – What the FK?
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7080294/

tacrolimus-label — acute afferent vasoconstriction in prescribing information:
https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=4caa20af-45a8-4365-9f06-1568afae61b1&type=display
