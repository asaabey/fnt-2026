DIURETIC MECHANISMS AND TERMINOLOGY
Prepared 11 November 2026. Source mechanisms checked against official labels
and renal physiology literature; the animation is an educational synthesis.

NEPHRON SITES
Site I: proximal tubule.
Site II: thick ascending limb (traditionally medullary diluting segment).
Site III: distal convoluted tubule (traditionally cortical diluting segment).
Site IV: late distal / connecting tubule and collecting system.

The four-site numbering remains present in teaching, but it is a schematic
classification rather than a unique modern anatomical nomenclature. This
animation gives the segment names prominence and site numbers secondary
status. Mannitol spans water-permeable segments; site numbers alone also do
not distinguish MR, ENaC and V2 mechanisms in the distal/collecting system.
