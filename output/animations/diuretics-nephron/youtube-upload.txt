TITLE
Mechanism of Diuretics | Sites of Action, Transporters and Receptors

DESCRIPTION
Explore how the major diuretic classes work using one nephron diagram, with animated insets showing each drug’s site of action and molecular target.

This video covers:
• Mannitol — osmotic diuresis
• Acetazolamide — carbonic anhydrase inhibition
• SGLT2 inhibitors — sodium–glucose cotransport inhibition
• Loop diuretics — NKCC2 blockade
• Thiazide and thiazide-like diuretics — NCC blockade
• Spironolactone and eplerenone — mineralocorticoid receptor antagonism
• Amiloride and triamterene — ENaC blockade
• Tolvaptan — vasopressin V2 receptor antagonism

Follow the links between tubular transport, salt and water excretion, potassium sparing, and urine-concentrating ability. The final comparison distinguishes natriuresis from aquaresis and shows how V2 signalling regulates AQP2 water channels.

Designed for medical students and renal physiology revision. The animation is silent, with on-screen explanations. Anatomical segment names are accompanied by traditional Site I–IV labels. Anatomy and transport are schematic.
