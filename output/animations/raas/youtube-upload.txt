TITLE
RAAS Explained | Renin, Angiotensin II and Aldosterone Animation

DESCRIPTION
Follow the renin–angiotensin–aldosterone system (RAAS), from the signals that trigger renin release to the effects of aldosterone on sodium and potassium handling.

This animation shows reduced renal perfusion, reduced macula-densa NaCl transport and sympathetic beta-1 signalling stimulating renin release. Follow the conversion of angiotensinogen to angiotensin I and angiotensin II, then see how vasoconstriction, proximal sodium reabsorption and adrenal aldosterone release support blood pressure and sodium conservation.

A principal-cell view connects apical ENaC and basolateral Na+/K+-ATPase activity with sodium reabsorption and potassium secretion. The closing scenes show negative feedback, direct stimulation of aldosterone by elevated potassium, and a separate example of autonomous aldosterone secretion with suppressed renin.

A silent animation with on-screen labels for medical students and renal physiology revision. Anatomy and ion movement are schematic. The final example illustrates hypertension with hypokalaemia; low potassium is not present in every case of primary aldosteronism.
